The seven realms of the Oathlands

The Oathlands is the archipelago at the heart of the epic fantasy Oathlands: The Death of a God, a story of a dying god-king, four rival gods and a blacksmith with debts to pay. This interactive 3D map lets you fly over every island, from marble cities to forge-smoke hills.

  • Caelmar, marble and law: a realm of white stone, tidy streets and unbroken order, seat of the god Caelthar.
  • The Far Waste, empty and untracked country: wind, dust and long silences, where maps stop being sure of themselves.
  • Aurendell, where the god-king keeps court: a terraced island of pale stone stepping down to the harbor, with a great hall at its heart.
  • Elowmere, orchard and river country: gentle, fertile and well fed, home of Sylvaine, god of mercy and harvest.
  • The Hollow Roads, lamplit crossroads: roads that meet in the dark, each crossing marked by a lamp, Oriel's country.
  • The Iron Country, hills of forge and coal-smoke: rough hills, hard people and honest work, and the town of Hearthmoor where the story begins.
  • The Drowned Coast, harbor isles of Morvaen: scattered islands where the tide sets the hours.
